Figure 1. Share of assistant turns in each reasoning-channel state, per model arm, over three safety benchmarks (sycophancy, xstest, strong_reject; n = 1,263 assistant turns per arm, identical 1,013 samples). Visibility is a partition, not a spectrum: five arms are readable at about 1.0; deepseek-v3.2 in its default configuration is fully absent (no reasoning produced) while the same model with reasoning requested is 0.980 readable; and gpt-5-nano at low, medium and high effort is fully redacted (encrypted). What an evaluator can read is set by the provider and the request, not by the model. Source: results/observability_record_reasoning.json.
