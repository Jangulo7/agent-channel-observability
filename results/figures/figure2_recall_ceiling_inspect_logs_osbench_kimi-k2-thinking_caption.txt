Figure 2. Measured positional coverage c(j) and the recall ceiling it imposes on a monitor gated on deliberation, with a perfect second stage (r₂ = 1). n = 479 assistant turns across 10 positions. Coverage is lowest at position 0 (1.000), so a monitor gated on deliberation cannot exceed 1.000 recall against an action taken there. The green dotted series is AdaptR1's per-step no-think ratio (arXiv:2605.31062, Table 4, MuSiQue, lambda=0.9), plotted as a reference point only: it is INDUCED by an RL reward that pays for first-step no-think and is manipulable across its whole range by the lambda term, not observed under no intervention. It is plotted in its own panel, on its own step axis: their step 2 and our position 2 are not the same quantity. AdaptThink's task spread (arXiv:2505.13417: gsm8k 0.996, math500 0.766, aime 0.063) is not plotted here because it varies by task, not by step position, and is likewise induced.
